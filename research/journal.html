About the Journal

The Bangladesh Journal of Refugee Law and Displacement Studies (BJRLDs) is dedicated to advancing critical scholarship and interdisciplinary research concerning refugee law, forced displacement, statelessness, human rights, and migration.

The Journal seeks to encourage academic dialogue among scholars, researchers, practitioners, policymakers, and students working on contemporary issues of displacement and protection.

SUBMISSION GUIDELINES

FOR AUTHORS

Submission Guidelines

Authors are encouraged to prepare their manuscripts carefully and ensure that submissions meet the Journal's academic and ethical standards.

Manuscripts should be original scholarly work.

Submissions should follow appropriate academic formatting and citation standards.

Authors should provide accurate institutional and contact information.

Submitted work should comply with the Journal's publication ethics.

ACADEMIC INTEGRITY

Publication Ethics

BJRLDs is committed to maintaining high standards of academic integrity, originality, transparency, and responsible scholarly communication.

Authors, reviewers, editors, and other contributors are expected to uphold ethical standards throughout the publication process and to ensure that scholarly work is presented accurately and responsibly.

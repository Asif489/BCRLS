Call for Blog Writing

The Bangladesh Centre for Refugee Law Studies (BCRLS) invites original blog submissions for its academic blog Non-Refoulement from students, academics, researchers, legal professionals, and scholars who are interested in refugee law, forced displacement, statelessness, and contemporary humanitarian challenges.

Refugee law is evolving rapidly in response to changing global realities. Armed conflicts, climate-induced displacement, border governance, statelessness, and gaps in international and domestic legal frameworks continue to raise complex legal and policy questions.

Through this blog initiative, BCRLS seeks to create a platform for informed, critical, and solution-oriented discussions that contribute to the development of refugee law and policy.

We particularly encourage submissions that move beyond description and engage with limitations, inconsistencies, and implementation challenges of existing refugee law frameworks while proposing practical legal, institutional, and policy reforms capable of addressing contemporary displacement challenges.

Suggested Research Themes

Areas of Interest

Contributors may explore a wide range of legal, policy, institutional, and interdisciplinary issues related to refugees and forced displacement.

Gaps in international refugee law.

The 1951 Refugee Convention and contemporary challenges.

Statelessness and nationality laws.

Climate-induced displacement and legal protection.

Non-refoulement and State responsibility.

Refugee rights and protection in South Asia.

Rohingya protection and legal governance.

Refugee status determination and access to justice.

Gender, children, disability, and vulnerability.

Digital identity, surveillance, and refugee rights.

Emerging legal reforms and innovative protection mechanisms.

Submission Guidelines

Preparing Your Blog

Word Count

Submissions should be between 800–1500 words.

Language

Blog submissions must be written in English.

Originality

Submissions must be original and unpublished.

Citation Style

Please use APA 7th edition in-text citations.

References

A complete APA 7th edition reference list should accompany the submission.

Author Biography

Include a short author biography of 50–80 words.

Submission Process

Submit Your Contribution

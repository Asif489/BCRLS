Founder’s Message

Building Knowledge, Advancing Refugee Protection

Welcome to the Bangladesh Centre for Refugee Law Studies (BCRLS).

BCRLS is committed to advancing research, education and informed dialogue on refugee law, forced displacement, migration and human rights.

Knowledge, dialogue and collaboration are essential to building a stronger understanding of refugee protection and human rights.

Through research, publications, education, seminars, discussions and collaboration, BCRLS seeks to create meaningful opportunities for students, researchers, academics, legal professionals and other stakeholders to engage with important questions surrounding displacement and protection.

We believe that sustained academic engagement and knowledge-sharing can contribute to better understanding, stronger institutions and more rights-based approaches to refugee protection.

We invite students, researchers, practitioners, institutions and interested individuals to engage with BCRLS and contribute to this important conversation.

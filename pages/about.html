WHO WE ARE

Building knowledge. Advancing justice.

BCRLS is a research and knowledge platform focused on refugee law, forced displacement and related questions of rights, policy and justice.

Through research, education and dialogue, BCRLS aims to connect evidence with meaningful action and contribute to informed discussion on issues affecting refugees, displaced communities and other vulnerable populations.

The Centre seeks to provide a space where scholars, researchers, students, practitioners, policymakers and other stakeholders can engage with important questions of refugee law and displacement.

Its work is intended to strengthen knowledge, encourage interdisciplinary research and promote greater understanding of the legal, social and policy dimensions of displacement.

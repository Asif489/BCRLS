What We Aim To Do

Our Mission

The Bangladesh Centre for Refugee Law Studies (BCRLS) is committed to advancing knowledge, research and informed dialogue on refugee law, forced displacement, migration and human rights.

Our mission is to create a platform where students, researchers, academics, legal professionals and other stakeholders can engage meaningfully with issues relating to refugee protection and human rights.

Research & Knowledge

Promote research, scholarship and knowledge-sharing on refugee law, displacement, migration and protection.

Education & Capacity Building

Support learning, academic engagement and capacity building for students, researchers and practitioners.

Refugee Protection

Contribute to informed understanding and stronger approaches to refugee protection and human rights.

Collaboration

Encourage collaboration among academics, researchers, legal professionals, institutions and wider communities.

We seek to build a stronger culture of research, dialogue and learning around refugee law and protection.

Our Vision

A Leading Platform for Refugee Law & Protection

The aim is to advance excellence in legal education in refugee law through rigorous academic instruction and the cultivation of critically informed scholars and practitioners.

It seeks to establish a leading platform for high-quality research on emerging and complex issues in refugee law, particularly within regional contexts.

01 · Legal Education

Advance excellence in legal education in refugee law through rigorous academic instruction and the cultivation of critically informed scholars and practitioners.

02 · High-Quality Research

Establish a leading platform for high-quality research on emerging and complex issues in refugee law, particularly within regional contexts.

03 · Policy & Legal Reform

Amplify informed and principled voices for refugees, contributing to meaningful policy discourse and legal reform.

04 · Scholarly Collaboration

Enhance intellectual engagement through sustained scholarly collaboration and interdisciplinary dialogue.

05 · Refugee Rights

Maintain a firm commitment to advocacy for the protection and promotion of refugee rights in accordance with international legal standards and humanitarian principles.

A firm commitment to excellence in legal education, high-quality research, meaningful policy discourse, scholarly collaboration and the protection and promotion of refugee rights.

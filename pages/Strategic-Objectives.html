Strategic Objectives

BCRLS seeks to translate its mission and vision into practical and sustainable activities that strengthen research, learning and informed engagement on refugee law, displacement, migration and human rights.

The following strategic objectives provide a framework for the Centre’s academic, research and collaborative activities.

01 · Research

Promote rigorous and interdisciplinary research on refugee law, forced displacement, migration and human rights.

02 · Publications

Encourage and disseminate academic publications, research findings and knowledge resources relating to refugee protection.

03 · Education

Create opportunities for students and emerging researchers to develop knowledge and skills in refugee law and protection.

04 · Community & Engagement

Facilitate meaningful discussions, seminars, workshops and other activities that promote informed public and academic engagement.

05 · Collaboration

Build partnerships with universities, researchers, legal professionals, institutions and relevant organizations.

06 · Protection & Rights

Contribute to informed, rights-based approaches to refugee protection, migration and human dignity.

Our strategic objectives connect research, education and collaboration with the broader goal of strengthening knowledge and refugee protection.

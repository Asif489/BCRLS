Our Team

Meet the academics, legal professionals, researchers and leaders who guide BCRLS in advancing refugee law through research, education, dialogue and institutional engagement.

ACADEMIC & STRATEGIC GUIDANCE

Board of Advisors

The Board of Advisors of BCRLS comprises distinguished academics, legal professionals, and subject-matter experts who provide strategic guidance and intellectual support to the organization. Their experience and insights play a vital role in shaping our vision, strengthening our initiatives, and ensuring the quality and impact of our research and programs. They serve as mentors and thought leaders, offering valuable advice on policy direction, research priorities, and institutional development. Through their continuous engagement, the Board of Advisors helps BCRLS uphold excellence, integrity, and innovation in all its activities.

LEADERSHIP & GOVERNANCE

Board of Directors

The Board of Directors of BCRLS serves as the core decision-making body of the organization, responsible for setting strategic direction, overseeing operations, and ensuring effective governance. Comprising dedicated individuals with strong leadership, academic, and organizational experience, the Board plays a pivotal role in driving the mission and long-term vision of BCRLS. They are actively involved in planning and implementing programs, managing institutional affairs, and maintaining accountability and transparency in all activities. Through their commitment and collaborative efforts, the Board of Directors ensures that BCRLS continues to grow as a dynamic platform for legal research, advocacy, and academic excellence.

OPERATIONS & IMPLEMENTATION

Operational Team

The Operational Team supports the day-to-day activities, research coordination, programs, communication and institutional operations of BCRLS.

About the Call

BCRLS welcomes original and unpublished research related to refugee law, forced displacement, migration and human rights.

Research Areas

  • Refugee Law and International Protection
  • Forced Displacement and Migration
  • Human Rights and Refugee Protection
  • Statelessness and Citizenship
  • Climate Change and Human Mobility

Submission Requirements

  • Original and unpublished research.
  • Clear research question and argument.
  • Proper academic citations and references.
  • Manuscript submitted in PDF or DOC/DOCX.

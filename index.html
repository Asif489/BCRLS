FOUNDER'S MESSAGE

A Message from the Founder

Welcome to the Bangladesh Centre for Refugee Law Studies. BCRLS is envisioned as a platform for research, education, dialogue and knowledge sharing on refugee law and displacement.

Our commitment is to encourage meaningful academic engagement, strengthen knowledge and contribute to a deeper understanding of the challenges surrounding displacement and human dignity.
